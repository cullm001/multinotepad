✍️ About MultiNote

MultiNote is a free online notepad that works right in your browser. No account needed — just open the page and start writing.

Multiple Notes & Folders — Create unlimited notes and organize them into folders. Drag and drop to reorder or move notes between folders. Search across all your content and track when each note was last edited.

Rich Text Formatting — Bold, italic, underline, lists, colors, fonts, and more. Format your notes exactly how you want them.

Auto-Save & Draft Recovery — Your notes save automatically as you type. Close your browser or navigate away? Your drafts are restored when you come back.

100% Private & Local — Everything stays on your device. No uploads, no servers, no accounts. Your notes never leave your browser.
